Our Services

Complete Electronic Design Solutions from Concept to Production

Electronics Design Expertise

At Prepreg Technologies Pvt Ltd., we provide complete hardware and software design services. Our engineering team supports customers from concept, design verification, PCB development, manufacturing support, and final product validation.

Our Design Services Include

  • Schematic Design Capture using OrCAD and Altium
  • Schematic Review & Verification
  • Component Selection and BoM Support
  • PCB Layout Design and Gerber Generation
  • High-Speed Multi-layer PCB Design
  • Signal Integrity (SI) Analysis
  • Power Integrity (PI) Analysis
  • Ansys Simulation and Thermal Analysis
  • FPGA Board Design and Development
  • RF, Digital and Analog Circuit Design
  • PCB Fabrication and CAM Support
  • PCB Assembly (PCBA) Support
  • Board Bring-up and Testing
  • Embedded Firmware Development
  • PCB Library & Footprint Development
  • Prototype to Production Support
