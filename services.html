Services

At Prepreg Technologies, we offer comprehensive electronics design services covering every stage from concept to production. Our expertise includes schematic capture, high-speed PCB design, mixed-signal board design, FPGA board design, and high-current PCB design.

We also provide advanced pre-design support such as Signal Integrity (SI) and Power Integrity (PI) analysis, along with Ansys simulations to ensure optimal performance. Our post-design services include fabrication and CAM support, bare board validation, and complete assembly assistance.

We offer design services which includes:

  • Schematic Design Capture
  • Schematic Review & Verification
  • Procurement & Assembly BoM Service
  • PCB Design – Gerber Generation
  • Signal Integrity
  • Power Integrity
  • FAB and CAM Support
  • PCBA Support
  • Board Bring-up and Testing
  • Embedded Software
  • Thermal Simulation
  • Web Development
  • RF / Digital and Analog Design
  • PCB Library (Footprint) Development
