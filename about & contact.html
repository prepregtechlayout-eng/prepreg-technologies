About Prepreg Technologies

Innovation, Quality and Engineering Excellence in Electronics Design

Who We Are

Founded in 2025, Prepreg Technologies Pvt Ltd provides complete electronics engineering services both in-house and onsite. We specialize in high-speed PCB design, mixed signal design, FPGA boards, embedded software, RF, digital and analog circuits. Our services extend from concept and simulation to fabrication support, assembly support, validation and production support.

Our Expertise

PCB Design

Multi-layer high-speed PCB layout, SI/PI analysis and manufacturing support.

Embedded Systems

Firmware development, hardware integration and complete embedded solutions.

FPGA Solutions

Advanced FPGA hardware design for AI, automation and industrial applications.
